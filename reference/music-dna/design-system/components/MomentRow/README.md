# MomentRow

A saved or suggested passage in a list: play it, see where it sits in its song, and what you said about it.

- Round play button · mini waveform (40 bars, 150px, the moment's span in `accent`) · song — artist (+ "New" in `accent` for fresh saves) · what it is and your note · tags · range · duration.
- Below `breakpoint-phone` drop the mini waveform and range.
- Discover uses the same row with a genre tag, a "why" line ("A voice rises over a held string drone. Similar to 4 of your moments in vocal transitions.") and That's it / Not quite instead of duration.
- Playing a moment from a list opens it in the player with the passage selected, so every list leads back to the same screen.
