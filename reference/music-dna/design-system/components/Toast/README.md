# Toast

A short confirmation at the bottom centre that says what just happened and offers one way to see it.

- `bg-raise`, `line-strong` border, `radius-md`, `shadow-pop`; `role="status"`. Disappears after 5 s.
- One sentence naming the result and where it went ("Saved to My Moments · added to vocal transitions"), plus at most one action in `accent` text (View, See Music DNA).
- Placed above the playback bar on desktop and above the tab bar on phones; never covers the waveform.
